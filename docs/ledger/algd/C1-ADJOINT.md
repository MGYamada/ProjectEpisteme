- Direction conventions: alg-d writes g ∘ f and GF, which are mathlib's f ≫ g and F ⋙ G. The unit is η : id_C ⇒ GF (mathlib `adj.unit : 𝟭 C ⟶ F ⋙ G`) and the counit is ε : FG ⇒ id_D (`adj.counit : G ⋙ F ⟶ 𝟭 D`). In 命題 28 and 系 29, F^{-1} is precomposition (mathlib `whiskeringLeft`), and the order reverses: G^{-1} ⊣ F^{-1}. In the CCC section the exponential is defined for − × a (product on the right), while mathlib's `MonoidalClosed`/`ihom` is defined for `tensorLeft A` (A ⊗ −). For cartesian structures, any bridge goes through the braiding.
