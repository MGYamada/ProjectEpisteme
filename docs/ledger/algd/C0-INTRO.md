## Notes

- Numbering: one shared counter, 1–32, all present. 命題 8 is the only non-example numbered item (31 examples, 1 proposition). No displayed equation carries a number in this PDF.
- Unnumbered `定義.` blocks: 圏 (D01), 関手 (D02), 同型射・同型 (D03), 反変関手 (D07), 反転圏 (D08). D04 (inverse f⁻¹), D05 (isomorphism of categories) and D06 (locally small) are defined in prose. The informal terms 忘却関手 (例 4), 恒等関手 and 合成関手 (例 18) and 共変関手 (D07) are recorded inside those rows rather than as separate D rows.
- Section 1 (p.1–2) is motivation: "isomorphic groups have equinumerous underlying sets" and "homeomorphic spaces have isomorphic fundamental groups", generalised to "a functor F : Top → C can detect non-homeomorphism". These are instances of 命題 8 and are covered by 例 9 and 例 10; they get no separate rows.
- The paragraph after 例 10 (p.6) mentions homology and cohomology (footnote 4) as further such functors. This is motivational and gets no row.
- Size and foundations: the notes treat "all sets" (例 1) and "all categories" (例 18) naively and suggest Grothendieck universes as one rigorous method (remark after 例 18). Project rules forbid a Grothendieck universe axiom, so these must be read with Lean's universe hierarchy: Set ↔ `Type u`, CAT ↔ `Cat.{v, u}` (which is not an object of itself). The global convention "every category is locally small" (D06) corresponds to `Category.{v, u}` with hom types in `Type v`.
- Composition order: the source writes g ∘ f (and GF for functors). mathlib writes `f ≫ g` and `F ⋙ G`. For a monoid viewed as a category (例 20) the source does not state the order of multiplication explicitly; the reading g ∘ f := g·f is forced by 例 25 (F(mn) = F m ∘ F n gives a left action) and matches `SingleObj.comp_as_mul : f ≫ g = g * f`.
- `CategoryTheory.IsIso` is a Prop; `CategoryTheory.Iso` is data. `CategoryTheory.inv` picks the inverse noncomputably from `IsIso`. The source's notion (D03) is existence, closest to `IsIso` and `IsIsomorphic` (= `Nonempty (X ≅ Y)`).
- Isomorphism of categories (D05) is used in 例 19 and 例 28 and later in the construction PDF (Set/1 ≅ Set, 1/Top ≅ Top∗). mathlib mostly provides equivalences `≌` for such statements; the rows say so where it matters.
- Text extraction: the 例 15 pictures and the C(−, ℂ) diagram before 定義 (反変関手) were garbled in the text. They were read from the PDF (p.7 and p.12). In 例 15 the second picture is a → b ← c with f : a → b and g : c → b.
