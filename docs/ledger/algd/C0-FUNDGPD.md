## Notes

- Numbering: the counter runs from 1 to 11 without gaps: 例 1–3, 命題 4, 定理 5, 命題 6–8, 系 9, 定理 10–11. No displayed equation carries a counter label.
- The notation I := [0, 1] is introduced in the opening prose (p.1) and is folded into ALGD-C0-FUNDGPD-D01.
- Composition order:
  - The source's q ∗ p runs through p first, and [q] ∘ [p] := [q ∗ p].
  - mathlib's `Path.trans p q` also runs through p first, so q ∗ p is `p.trans q` and [q] ∘ [p] is `[p] ≫ [q]` in `FundamentalGroupoid X`.
  - `FundamentalGroup.mul_def` (p * q is `q.trans p`) and `CategoryTheory.End` multiplication both follow function composition, which matches the source's ∘.
  - `CategoryTheory.Groupoid.vertexGroup` multiplies in diagrammatic order (x * y = x ≫ y), so its group structure is opposite to `End`.
- Garbled text checked against the PDF:
  - The bar on p̄ in 例 3 and in the proof of 命題 4 is lost in the text extraction; the definition reads p̄(t) := p(1 − t).
  - The square in 定理 10 and 定理 11 has π1(U ∩ V, −) at the top left, π1(V, −) at the top right, π1(U, −) at the bottom left and π1(X, −) at the bottom right. The arrows carry no labels; they are taken to be induced by the inclusions.
- Claims the source asserts without proof:
  - ∼ is an equivalence relation (R01).
  - Composition in Π(X) is well defined and satisfies the category axioms (inside D04).
  - Π(F) is well defined on homotopy classes (R02).
  - 定理 10 and 定理 11 are cited, with reference [1] (クゼ・コスニオフスキ『トポロジー入門』, 1983).
  - These form the main topological prerequisites, together with the pasting lemma (例 2), the product topology on I² and path components (定理 10).
- 定理 10: A is an arbitrary set; π1(X, A) uses the objects X ∩ A. The hypothesis covers only the path components of U, V and U ∩ V.
- Dependencies on other PDFs:
  - intro.pdf: the fundamental group as a motivating example, monoids as one-object categories (ALGD-C0-INTRO-20), and CAT (ALGD-C0-INTRO-18).
  - construction.pdf: full subcategories.
- Choice: the source uses none explicitly. In mathlib, `PathConnectedSpace.somePath` (behind 系 9) is noncomputable.
- Footnote *1 notes that 亜群 is also used for a set with a binary operation (a magma). That sense is not used here.
