## Notes
- Numbering: one shared counter, 1–8, all present (例 1–5, 命題 6, 命題 7, 例 8). The numbered lists (1)–(3) inside the definition and (1), (2) inside 命題 7 are local labels and do not use the counter. No exercises.
- Kept inside numbered items, without separate D/R rows: the category of elements and the isomorphism (1 ↓ P)^op ≅ y ↓ P are part of 例 5 (the example ends with □ after that sentence on p.3); Graph is part of 例 4.
- Direction conventions checked against the PDF: the morphism condition is Gg1 ∘ f = f′ ∘ Fg0, matching mathlib's `L.map left ≫ hom' = hom ≫ R.map right`. In y ↓ P a morphism ⟨c, x⟩ → ⟨c′, x′⟩ is f : c → c′ with Pf(x′) = x (contravariant action), while in 1 ↓ P it is a C^op-morphism, that is, f : c′ → c in C with Pf(x) = x′.
- mathlib uses `Discrete PUnit` for the one-object category 1 and `Type u` for Set. In 例 5 the functor 1 : 1 → Set picks a one-element set. mathlib's `StructuredArrow PUnit F` uses the type `PUnit` as the object of `Type`.
- 命題 6 uses "=" (identification by swapping components). mathlib has `Comma.opEquiv` in the form `Comma L R ≌ (Comma R.op L.op)ᵒᵖ`, the opposite side of the source's statement, so applying it needs an `op`/`unop` bridge.
- 命題 7 states equalities of functors. When formalizing it, decide whether to keep strict equality (provable here because H is defined componentwise) or move to an up-to-isomorphism statement. Footnote *4 says the source considers the 2-categorical (pseudo) version the "correct" one and treats it in 「2-category での極限」 (chapter 5).
- Cross-references to other PDFs: 「圏の構成例」 (construction.pdf: slice category C/a, products), 「自然変換・関手圏」 (nat.pdf: arrow category C^2, whiskering κH), 「米田の補題」 (yoneda.pdf: Yoneda embedding and lemma), 「2-category での極限」 (chapter 5).
- Verification (2026-11-26): 9 rows are verified in `ProjectEpisteme/Category/AlgD/C1/Comma.lean` (namespace `ProjectEpisteme.AlgD.CommaCategory`), and 例 4 is non-propositional. Every declaration depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`comma_category` does not even use `Classical.choice`), pinned with `#guard_msgs` and checked again by `checks/AxiomAudit.lean`.
- Isomorphisms and identifications of categories (例 1, 2, 3, 5 and 命題 6) are stated as `IsoCat` or `Functor.IsIso`, not as mathlib's equivalences. For 命題 6, mathlib's `Comma.opEquiv : Comma L R ≌ (Comma R.op L.op)ᵒᵖ` faces the other way; the Lean statement builds `IsoCat (F ↓ G)ᵒᵖ (G^op ↓ F^op)` directly from `Comma.opFunctor` and `Comma.unopFunctor`.
- 命題 7 keeps the source's strict equalities of functors. mathlib has no general lift into a comma category (only `MorphismProperty.Comma.lift` and the special cases `Functor.toStructuredArrow` and `Functor.toCostructuredArrow`), so `Comma.liftFunctor` is added in the library module `ProjectEpisteme/Category/Basic/Comma.lean`.
- `Comma.snd` is generated from `Comma.fst` by `to_dual`. The old `CategoryOfElements.*` names in mathlib are deprecated in favour of `Functor.Elements.*`.
