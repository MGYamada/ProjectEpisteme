## Notes
- Numbering: one shared counter, 1–13, all present (例 1–8, 命題 9, 例 10–13). There are no equation labels and no exercises. Section numbers (§1 自然変換の計算, §2 関手圏) are separate from the counter.
- The file has no `定義.` blocks; every definition is in prose. Terms defined inside numbered examples get no separate D row: horizontal composition (例 2), interchange law (例 3), diagonal functor (例 10), ev_a and ev (例 11), arrow category (例 12). 例 2 includes the equality σF1 ∘ G0θ = G1θ ∘ σF0, because its □ comes after that argument on p.6.
- Whiskering names: the source's Hθ (functor on the left in ∘ notation) is mathlib's `Functor.whiskerRight θ H`, and the source's θF is `Functor.whiskerLeft F θ`. Composition: source σ ∘ θ is mathlib `θ ≫ σ`, and source GF is `F ⋙ G`. mathlib's `NatTrans.hcomp θ σ` (θ ◫ σ) has the component formula G1θ ∘ σF0, the other side of 例 2's equality.
- Overloaded notation: the source writes F (not F_*) for postcomposition C^M → D^M (D05) and F^{-1} for precomposition M^D → M^C (D06). adjoint.pdf (命題 27, 28) relies on this.
- The ※ box after 例 3 (p.7) says general pasting coherence is deferred to 「2-category」. It is recorded here only as a cross-reference and gets no row.
- Size and universes: R01 is the only place where local smallness of D^C is discussed. The rest of the notes treat D^C as locally small. In Lean, `C ⥤ D` has hom types in `Type (max u₁ v₂)`, which is `Type v` for `SmallCategory C` and `Category.{v} D` at the same universe.
- 例 4 and 例 5: in Lean, `𝟭 C ⋙ F` and `F` are definitionally equal by structure eta but not syntactically equal, so equalities of natural transformations across them may need `Functor.leftUnitor`/`rightUnitor` or `eqToHom`.
- Cross-references to other PDFs: 「例: 位相空間上の層」 (sheaf.pdf: presheaves, 例 8), the earlier treatment of monoids as categories and M-sets (例 6), and 「2-category」 (general pasting).
- Verification (2026-11-26): 22 rows are verified in `ProjectEpisteme/Category/AlgD/C1/Nat.lean`, and 例 1 is non-propositional (its composite is type-checked by an `example`). Every declaration depends only on `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`, pinned with `#guard_msgs` in the module and checked again by `checks/AxiomAudit.lean`.
- Isomorphisms of categories: the source writes `≅` for an isomorphism of categories and `≃` for an equivalence (「自然変換・圏同値」). 例 5, 6, 7, 12 and 13 therefore use `Functor.IsIso` (full, faithful, bijective on objects), which assembles into mathlib's `IsoCat`, rather than mathlib's equivalences. The needed functors and instances are in the library module `ProjectEpisteme/Category/Basic/FunctorCategory.lean`.
- Modelling choices: the two-element discrete category {a, b} of 例 7 is `Discrete Bool`; the category 2 = {0 → 1} of 例 12 and 13 is `Fin 2` with its order; P(X) in 例 13 is `Set X` ordered by inclusion, and χ uses classical decidability.
- Source typo in 例 4 (p.7): the rightmost component is written (θ_F ∘ Fσ)_a. With σ first and θ second, the horizontal composite is θ_G ∘ Fσ, which is what the Lean statement uses.
- The anonymous `LocallySmall` instance for functor categories is in Mathlib/CategoryTheory/EssentiallySmall.lean. `Arrow.rightFunc` is generated from `Arrow.leftFunc` by `to_dual`.
