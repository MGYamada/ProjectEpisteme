## Notes

- Numbering: the shared counter numbers the two displayed diagrams (1) (p.2) and (2) (p.2) before 命題 3. Following the project rule, they are rows 01 and 02 of kind `equation`, and each states the commutativity claim attached to it. 命題 3 is the only numbered statement. There are no 定理, 系, 補題, 注意 or 例 items.
- Exercises: the PDF has two unnumbered 練習問題 on p.6. They are given IDs `-E01` and `-E02` in order of appearance. Neither is solved in the PDF.
- Garbled text resolved from the PDF: the pictures of diagram (1), the triangle, diagram (2), the (∗)-labelled version of (2), the 命題 3 rectangle, the diagrammatic proof (p.5) and both exercise diagrams (p.6) were unreadable in the text extraction and were read from the PDF pages. For E01, the placement of the five (∗) faces was read from the picture: a₂a₃b₃b₂, a₃a₄b₄b₃, the hexagon a₁a₂b₂c₂c₁b₁, the right region bounded by b₃ → b₄ → a₄ → a₅ → d₅ → d₃ and b₃ → b₂ → c₂ → c₃ → d₃, and the lower region bounded by c₂ → c₁ → b₁ → b₀ → d₀ → d₃ and c₂ → c₃ → d₃. With this reading the outer equation follows by pasting the five faces in turn, which supports the reading.
- 命題 3 only needs f to be an epimorphism. The source assumes an isomorphism and composes with f⁻¹. A Lean version could state the source form and note the epi generalisation via `cancel_epi`.
- p.5 explains the notes' convention of writing proofs as equalities of diagrams. It is presentation only and has no row.
- The informal notion of diagram (D01) differs from mathlib's use of "diagram" for a functor J ⥤ C. Limits PDFs later use the functor sense; this PDF only uses the picture sense.
- Cross-reference: p.1 points to 「圏論とは何か」 (ALGD-C0-INTRO) for earlier uses of diagrams. 命題 3 uses isomorphisms and inverses from ALGD-C0-INTRO-D03 and -D04.
