- Text extraction: pdftotext renders ⟨ ⟩ as `h … i`, splits ≅ into `∼` and `=` on separate lines, and garbles ∐, ∏, T̃ and Ĉ. The diagrams for (2), (6), 例 23 (p.21), 定理 31 (p.26) and 例 33 (pp.27–28) were read from the PDF.
- Pages: 命題 8 (p.6) and 定義 (余連続) (p.25) begin on lines that start with a form feed and were checked against the PDF.
- Conventions: composition is written g ∘ f (mathlib f ≫ g). A limit is a universal arrow from ∆ to T, that is, a terminal object of ∆ ↓ T ↔ `CostructuredArrow (Functor.const J) T`. A colimit is a universal arrow from T to ∆, that is, an initial object of T ↓ ∆ ↔ `StructuredArrow T (Functor.const J)`; mathlib's `Cone.equivCostructuredArrow` and `Cocone.equivStructuredArrow` match these directions.
- Isomorphism versus equivalence: 命題 5, 7 and 8 assert isomorphisms of categories T ↓ ∆ ≅ Q_fg, R_fg, P_ab. mathlib only supplies equivalences (`Cocone.equivStructuredArrow`), and its cocone categories keep the forced legs (θ_i) that the source's Q_fg and R_fg omit. The Lean module defines P_ab, Q_fg and R_fg as in the source and proves that the comparison functors from T ↓ ∆ are isomorphisms of categories (`Functor.IsIso`).
- Source typos: 例 9 writes {a_i}_{j∈J} for {a_j}_{j∈J}. 例 11 says "任意の n ∈ N" where n > 0 is meant, and "C の射 h" where a map of sets is meant.

- Cross-PDF dependencies: 「普遍性」 and 「双対」 (Chapter 0, ALGD-C0-UNIV, ALGD-C0-DUAL); 「コンマ圏」 (comma categories c ↓ G, T ↓ ∆, 1 ↓ F); 「自然変換・関手圏」 (the functor category C^J and the currying T ↦ T̃ used in 定理 20, 例 23, 命題 32); 「米田の補題」 (命題 24, 定理 25, ALGD-C1-YONEDA); 「フィルター圏」 (footnote 5); and 「双対」 命題 18 (epi ⇔ pushout square, ALGD-C0-DUAL-18), used in 例 33.
