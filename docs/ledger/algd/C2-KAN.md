The source index first gave 54 numbers. Cataloging found (55), and the index now gives 55. No exercises.
- Definitions: there are six `定義.` blocks, on p.1, p.15, p.17, p.18, p.37–38 and p.42. The block on p.37–38 defines both strongly generating functors (D09) and dense functors (D10). The prose definitions are the unit and the notation Lan (D02), right Kan extensions (D03), Kan lifts (D04), absolute right Kan extensions and absolute Kan lifts (D08), and the name "universal adjunction" (D11). Pointwise right Kan extensions appear in the ※ after 定理 25 (R03) without a separate definition.
- Not given rows: the motivating discussion before 定理 4 (p.4–6), the ※ after 定理 23 (a second proof of 定理 23 by hom-set bijections, p.25), the remark after 定理 36 that F†E extends E, and the ※ on p.49–51 (a second explanation of one step in the proof of 定理 54 by the 2-dimensional universality of comma categories).
- Direction conventions: the source extends E : C → U along F : C → D, while mathlib's `L.leftKanExtension F` extends F along L. The Lean statements keep the source's letters. The source's τF ∘ η is `η ≫ whiskerLeft F τ`, and F⁻¹ is precomposition `(whiskeringLeft C D U).obj F`. Kan lifts use postcomposition `(whiskeringRight X D U).obj F`. For absolute Kan lifts the test functors act by precomposition, so in 定理 21 and 22 "F commutes with F‡ id_D" means precomposition with F (with G in 定理 22).
- Universes: "absolute" quantifies over all categories V, but Lean can only quantify over one universe pair. That pair is given by the first two universe parameters of `IsAbsoluteLeftKanExtension` and its variants. In 定理 17, 20, 21 and 22, condition (2) uses the universes that (2) ⇒ (3) needs, those of D or C, and an adjunction gives absoluteness in every universe. For the same reason 命題 16 keeps the conjunct "is a left Kan extension". Sizes follow the source: 系 5 and R02 take a small C : Type v, with D and U of hom universe v. 系 29, R05 and 命題 32 hold for every locally small C, with Ĉ = C^op → Type v₁.
- 命題 1 writes representability out as a natural family of bijections, because Hom(E, SF) and Hom(T, S) lie in different universes in general.
- mathlib: `Functor.pointwiseLeftKanExtension` is the construction in the proof of 定理 4. `LeftExtension.IsPointwiseLeftKanExtension` is data (a family of `IsColimit`), so statements wrap it in `Nonempty`. `Functor.PreservesLeftKanExtension` quantifies over all left Kan extensions; the source's "commutes with ⟨F†E, η⟩" is `CommutesWith`. The bicategorical theorems `Bicategory.isLeftAdjoint_TFAE` and `Bicategory.isRightAdjoint_TFAE` are 定理 17 and 22 in a bicategory.
