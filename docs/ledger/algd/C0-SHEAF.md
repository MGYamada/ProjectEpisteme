## Notes

- Numbering: the counter runs from 1 to 8 without gaps. 例 1–6 and 命題 8 are items, and number 7 is the equation label of the displayed diagram (7) on p.5, which shares the counter. Following the ledger rule, it has its own row (ALGD-C0-SHEAF-07, kind `equation`, non-propositional), and 命題 8 uses it.
- Restriction-map indices: the source writes ρ_{UV} : P(V) → P(U) for U ⊂ V. Footnote *1 mentions the variants ρ_{VU}, ρ^V_U and r. The morphism U → V of O_X is written f_{VU}, and P(f_{VU}) = ρ_{UV}. In mathlib this is `F.map (homOfLE h).op` with `h : U ≤ V`.
- Not cataloged as rows, because no later item uses them:
  - The boxed ※ remark (pp.2–3). It says that 例 1's presheaf is a presheaf of commutative rings O_X^op → CRing, mentions presheaves of abelian groups and of ℂ-vector spaces, says "presheaf of sets" is used when a distinction is needed, and restricts attention to set-valued presheaves. In mathlib these correspond to `TopCat.presheafToTopCommRing` (Mathlib/Topology/Sheaves/CommRingCat.lean) and `TopCat.Presheaf CommRingCat X`.
  - The closing prose (p.6). It says that generalizing sheaves to a category needs Grothendieck topologies, to be treated in the 「トポス」 PDF.
- Footnote *2 explains the name 層 (French faisceau) and refers to 「例：位相空間上の層 その 2」 for pictures. It contains no mathematics.
- Garbled text checked against the PDF:
  - The product subscripts (∏_{i∈I}, ∏_{i,j∈I}).
  - The restriction subscripts ρ_{V_i∩V_j, V_i} and ρ_{V_i U}.
  - The case split in 例 4.
  - The equalizer diagrams in the proof of 命題 8 (pp.5–6).
- 例 6 silently needs U and V to be nonempty (otherwise f_U and f_V cannot have different values). The cover in question is {U, V} of U ∪ V.
- 例 5 is justified informally. Its statement about the C^∞ presheaf assumes the manifold structure of 例 3.
- Universes: the source allows arbitrary index sets I. mathlib's `IsSheafUniqueGluing` quantifies over `ι : Type x` (the universe of X), and `IsSheafEqualizerProducts` over `ι : Type v'`.
- Dependencies on other PDFs: equalizers and products (limit.pdf, by its file name); preorders as categories (ALGD-C0-INTRO-17); opposite categories (ALGD-C0-INTRO-D08). Choice: none, apart from the unique choice in the proof of 命題 8.
