## Notes
- Numbering: the counter runs 1–15 with no displayed equation labels. There are 9 `定義.` blocks (D01–D09) and no exercises. The proofs of 命題 1 use STEP 1–3, which are not numbered.
- Formulations: 1 ↓ F for F : C → Set is mathlib's category of elements `F.Elements` (equivalent to `StructuredArrow PUnit F`). y†F is `yoneda.leftKanExtension F : Ĉ → Set`, which exists for small C; flat (D04) and sifted flat (D07) are defined through it, as in the source. mathlib's `RepresentablyFlat` asks every d ↓ F to be cofiltered; for Set-valued F, 定理 7 relates the source's flatness to 1 ↓ F only. Sifted (D05) is the source's definition `IsSiftedSet`, shown equivalent to mathlib's `IsSifted`. Span(a, b) and Cospan(a, b) are comma categories over the diagonal (D09).
- Source issue: 命題 14 (and its dual R03) omits that C must be nonempty in (2) and (3). The empty category satisfies (2) and (3) vacuously, but colim of the empty diagram is ∅, which is not terminal, so it is not sifted. The source omits the proof of (2) ⇒ (1). mathlib's `IsSifted` includes `Nonempty C` for this reason.
- Proof routes that differ from the source: 定理 7 (1) ⇒ (2) passes through 1 ↓ (y†F), which is cofiltered because y†F preserves finite limits, and through a fully faithful embedding of 1 ↓ F into it; the source computes y†F on products and equalizers directly. (3) ⇒ (1) identifies y†F with colim_j ev_{Sj} as a left Kan extension along y, as the source does with y† ⊣ y⁻¹. 系 8 (⇒) is mathlib's direct proof. 命題 14 is proved from mathlib's description of sifted categories, not from 補題 13. These declarations are proved here from mathlib, since source-layer modules do not import the 「Kan拡張」 module.
- Deferred rows (second FILTERED batch): 例 12 (a counterexample in Top, which needs explicit colimits of spaces), 補題 13 and 定理 15 (sifted flat functors; 補題 13 needs the zigzag description of the colimit y†F(P) in Set). They are `unreviewed` with candidates recorded.
