## Notes

- Numbering: one shared counter, 1–12, all examples, all present. No displayed equation carries a number. There are no 定理, 命題, 系, 補題 or 注意 items and no exercises.
- Unnumbered `定義.` blocks: 直積 (D01), 直和 (D03), スライス圏 (D04), コスライス圏 (D05), 部分圏 (D06), 充満部分圏 (D07). The projection functors (D02) are defined in prose between 例 3 and 定義 (直和).
- The boxed remark inside 例 5 (Set/1 ≅ Set via F(f) := dom(f)) and the closing sentence ∅/Set ≅ Set are recorded in row 05 rather than as separate R rows, because the example's own conclusion is this isomorphism.
- Isomorphism versus equivalence: rows 01, 04, 05, 06 and 07 claim identifications or isomorphisms of categories (ALGD-C0-INTRO-D05). mathlib provides equivalences (`≌`) for all available cases. A Lean version must either state the equivalence and record the difference, or build an isomorphism in `Cat` (e.g. via `CategoryTheory.Cat.isoOfEquiv`), which needs strict object-level round trips.
- Subcategories (D06) are the main gap. mathlib's categories are types, so "Ob(C) ⊂ Ob(D)" cannot be a literal inclusion. Full subcategories (`ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory`) and wide subcategories (`WideSubcategory`) exist separately; a general subcategory structure was not found in the pin. Rows 08, 10 and 11 depend on this.
- Product category on `Fin 2 × Fin 2` (row 03): `CategoryTheory.prod'` and `Preorder.smallCategory` for the product preorder (`Prod`'s `Preorder` instance, Mathlib/Order/Basic.lean) both give category structures on the same type. The two agree up to the evident identification, but the Lean statement should fix the instance explicitly.
- Universes: `C × D` and `C ⊕ D` in mathlib have hom types in `Type (max v₁ v₂)`. `Over X` and `Under X` are comma categories (`CostructuredArrow`/`StructuredArrow`), so an object carries a trivial `Discrete PUnit` component besides the source's morphism f : a → x.
- Text extraction: the 2 × 2 picture in 例 3, the slice and coslice triangles and the 例 5 triangle were garbled in the text and were read from the PDF (p.2–4). "Top∗" in 例 7 was split across lines in the text; footnote 1 confirms it is the pointed-space category.
- Cross-PDF dependencies: discrete categories, posets, monoids and groups as categories, the categories 2 and 0, Top∗ and isomorphism of categories all come from 「圏論とは何か」 (ALGD-C0-INTRO). Commutativity in 例 3 and 例 5 uses 「可換図式について」 (ALGD-C0-CDIAG).
