## Notes

- Numbering: one shared counter, 1–9, all present (例 1, 例 2, 命題 3, 定理 4, 定理 5, 定理 6, 系 7, 例 8, 定理 9). There are no equation labels and no exercises.
- Definitions: five `定義.` blocks: wedge (D01), end (D02), cowedge (D03), coend (D04), and copower and power (one block, D05 and D06). The notation ∫_c and ∫^c is part of D02 and D04. The opening prose, which reads natural transformations as families 1 → Hom_D(Fa, Ga), is part of row 01.
- Formulations: the source's definitions are stated for T : C^op × C → D as `Cᵒᵖ × C ⥤ D` (`IsWedge`, `IsEnd`, `IsCowedge`, `IsCoend`) and bridged to mathlib's `Wedge` and `Cowedge` (multiforks) for `Functor.curryObj T`. Later rows use mathlib's curried form `Cᵒᵖ ⥤ C ⥤ D` directly. In 命題 3 the functor of three variables is curried with the parameter first. In 定理 4 and R01, T is `Cᵒᵖ × C × Xᵒᵖ × X ⥤ D`, regarded as a functor of ⟨c, x⟩ ∈ C × X (`fubiniProd`) and as a family indexed by X^op × X (`fubiniParam`). Copowers and powers are defined by their universal properties (`IsCopower`, `IsPower`); they are coproducts and products of constant families (`isCopower_iff_isColimit`, `isPower_iff_isLimit`), and 定理 9 and R06 take them in that form.
- Proof routes that differ from the source: 例 8 is checked from the universal properties rather than through the adjunctions, and R03 is proved from the coend's universal property rather than through T^op and 系 7. 定理 9 and R06 build the colimit over F ↓ d (the limit over d ↓ F) directly, rather than through the Hom computation and 「Kan拡張」's characterization of pointwise Kan extensions. The coend half of R01 is the end statement for T^op, as in the source's definition of coends.
- Second batch (2026-11-27): 定理 4 (Fubini), R01 (interchange of ends and of coends), 定理 9 (pointwise left Kan extensions as coends of copowers) and R06 (the dual end formula) are verified.
