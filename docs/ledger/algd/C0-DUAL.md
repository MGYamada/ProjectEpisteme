## Notes
- Numbering: one shared counter, 1–20, all present. Propositions: 1–3, 10, 11, 13–20. Examples: 4–9, 12. There are no numbered equation or diagram labels and no exercises.
- Proofs: 命題 1–3 and 17–20 are stated without proof ("reverse the arrows"); 命題 10 (1)–(2), 11 and 13–16 are proved.
- 命題 3 uses "C has coproducts / coequalizers / pushouts", which this PDF does not define. They are the duals of ALGD-C0-UNIV-D06 (binary coproducts).
- Diagrams read from the PDF (text extraction garbles them): in 命題 2 the top row is a → b → c and the bottom row x → y → z, with the left square the given pushout. 命題 14's square has id_a on the top and left edges and f on the right and bottom edges. 命題 16 is a →f b ⇉(g, h) c. 命題 19's square has c →g b at the top, f : c → a on the left, p1 : b → a ⨿_c b on the right and p0 : a → a ⨿_c b at the bottom. 命題 20 is a ⇉(g, h) b →f c.
- The unnumbered remark ※ on p.5 after 例 7 (in Grp, Ab and Top products are structured set products, but coproducts are not, Top excepted, explained in Chapter 1 by adjoints) is explanatory and is not cataloged as a formal claim.
- Footnote 2 defines "balanced" (mono plus epi implies iso). No later item relies on it, so it has no D row; the mathlib counterpart is `CategoryTheory.Balanced`, recorded in 例 12.
- `to_dual`-generated names (`BinaryCofan.IsColimit.mk`, `IsColimit.coconePointUniqueUpToIso`, `mono_comp`, `mono_of_mono`, `IsIso.mono_of_iso`, `epi_of_isColimit_cofork`, `IsColimit.op`, `op_mono_of_epi`) have no literal `def` line. Each was confirmed by use sites in the pinned checkout. The exception is `epi_to` (命題 17): only the attribute was found.
- Conventions: composition is written g ∘ f (mathlib f ≫ g). Pushout data ⟨a ⨿_c b, p0, p1⟩ for f : c → a, g : c → b matches mathlib's `IsPushout f g inl inr` with inl = p0 and inr = p1.
- 例 9: the source's reason, that a ring homomorphism ℚ → R is "determined by f(1)", holds because ring homomorphisms preserve 1; at most one such homomorphism exists. mathlib proves epi-ness for every localization.
